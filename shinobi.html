Six ways to lose a draft

Every chapter holds the same two drawings: a pencil wireframe on paper and the finished design underneath. A WebGL2 shader decides, pixel by pixel, which drawing you see, and your scroll drives the technique.

Shadow Clone · Multiply the draft
The sketch starts to tremble and its lines double, a shadow of itself pulling away. Then it splits: a puff of smoke covers the page and nine clones fly out of it, each a whole copy of the draft, stacked and overlapping. Keep scrolling and the clone on top goes up in smoke, showing whatever is under it, one after another, until only the finished design is left. Click a clone to dispel it early. Scroll back up and they come back. With sound on, every clone goes with a poof.
Sand Vortex · Weather the draft
Sand drifts in from the corners and grains lift off the darkest pencil strokes. Then a storm swirls round the page and wears the sketch away from the outside in, grain by grain along spiral arms, the paper going gritty ahead of it. Behind it the finished design shows through the swirling haze. At the end the storm closes into a ball of sand in the middle, and the ball bursts: the sand rains off the page and the air clears. Move over the paper and the sand eddies round your hand. Click to stir up a whirl that wears its own hole. With sound on, the storm roars and hisses, and the burst lands with a whump.
Spiral Sphere · Drill the draft
A sphere of light gathers in the middle of the page. Streams of light spiral into it, and the paper around it is dragged round into the spin. When it hits there is a flash and a shockwave, and it grinds into the page. The hole spreads as you scroll. Its edge saws round on spinning spiral arms, scoring and twisting the paper ahead of it. Behind it the finished design settles out of the twist, with rings of light running out from the sphere. Move over the paper and it twists under your hand. Click to drop a small sphere that drills its own hole. With sound on, the sphere whirs louder and higher as it charges, and grinds as it drills.
Lightning Blade · Cut the draft
The light drains from the page and a dense ball of blue-white lightning gathers low on the left, held down by the paper like a hand. It shrieks like a flock of birds while arcs spray off it and crawl over the paper, which scorches underneath. Then it charges across the page, dragged along the paper, gouging a burning trench with sparks spraying off the back, and thrusts straight through at the end with a flash. The trench widens as you scroll, its edge white-hot and flickering, with cracks of light running ahead of it. Move over the paper and static gathers under your hand. Click to call down a bolt that blows its own hole. With sound on, the shriek climbs as it charges and holds through the run, and the pierce lands with a thunderclap.
Sandstorm · Scour the draft
From the first scroll the wind is up: sand races across the page in gusts, bands of haze drift past, the paper shivers and the light goes orange-brown and dim. On the left a towering wall of dust looms, rust-dark at its base and wispy up high, its billows rolling forward. It bears down and sweeps across. Inside it you can barely see the page: streaks of sand race past and tear the sketch off grain by grain. Behind it the haze slowly thins, and the finished design is left standing. Move over the paper and the sand eddies round your hand. Click to kick up a dust devil that scours its own hole. With sound on, the wind howls and gusts from the first scroll, and the sand hisses harder the faster you scroll.
Sand Tornado · Uproot the draft
The light goes grey and a thin rope of sand reaches down from a dust cloud at the top of the page. It touches down in the middle with a thud and a burst of dust, then grows as you scroll, wider, taller and nearer, its bands of sand getting bigger as it comes. Its foot tears the paper away from the middle outward, along an edge that turns with it, and the scraps spiral up into the column. By the end it fills the screen and blows through in a blast of dust, and when the air clears only the finished design is left. Move over the paper and it swirls under your hand. Click to spin up a dust devil that tears its own patch. With sound on, the twister roars like a freight train, louder the nearer it gets and the harder you scroll, and lands with a thud.

Scroll back up in any chapter and the draft comes back. Each technique also has a page of its own.

The elemental pack Use it in your project

Raw WebGL2 and canvas, no libraries. Without WebGL2 it falls back to a wipe. With reduced motion on it cross-fades instead, with no flashes. Sound is off until you turn it on, and every sound is synthesised in the browser with Web Audio, so there are no audio files. One WebGL canvas draws every chapter, each with its own fragment shader.

Anime-inspired techniques, every effect written from scratch.
