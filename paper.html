Reveal · Paper pack

Three ways to lose a draft. Same sketch, same design, a different way through.

Every chapter holds the same two drawings: a pencil wireframe on paper and the finished design underneath. A WebGL2 shader works out, pixel by pixel, where each part of the sheet has moved to: rolled round a cylinder, flipped over a crease, or inked and washed.

01Page CurlTurn the draft The wireframe is a page bound at the left edge. Scroll turns it over like a magazine page, bending and catching the light, to uncover the finished design. Click to flick it. Open on its own → 02FoldFold the draft A pencil wireframe folds in half, then in half again, four times like origami, each fold uncovering more of the finished design, until the little folded packet tumbles off the page. Click to snap a crease. Open on its own → 03Ink DrawInk the draft A pen draws the finished design's outlines over a pencil wireframe in ink, the pencil fades away under it, and watercolour blooms into the inked shapes until the finished design is all that is left. Click to drop some ink. Open on its own →

Scroll back up in any chapter and the draft comes back. Each effect also has a page of its own.

The elemental pack Use it in your project

Raw WebGL2 and canvas, no libraries. Without WebGL2 it falls back to a wipe. With reduced motion on it cross-fades instead, with no flashes. Sound is off until you turn it on, and every sound is synthesised in the browser with Web Audio, so there are no audio files. One WebGL canvas draws every chapter, each with its own fragment shader.
