Reveal · Elemental pack

Six ways to lose a draft. Same sketch, same design, a different way through.

Every chapter holds the same two drawings: a pencil wireframe on paper and the finished design underneath. A WebGL2 shader gives each pixel a fuel value and decides, pixel by pixel, which drawing you see. Scrolling pushes the front through the fuel. Each element shapes that fuel differently and does different damage at the edge.

01FireBurn the draft Call down a comet and burn a pencil wireframe away to reveal the finished design. Throw fire at the paper, or scroll back up and call the comet again. Open on its own → 02WaterWash the draft Water pours down a pencil wireframe in rivulets that merge into a sheet, washing the sketch away to reveal the finished design. Click to spill some more. Open on its own → 03AirScatter the draft Wind strips a pencil wireframe off the page in streaks and carries it away, leaving the finished design. Click to send a gust. Open on its own → 04EarthBreak the draft A pencil wireframe cracks along jagged fault lines and falls apart chunk by chunk, revealing the finished design underneath. Click to start a quake. Open on its own → 05LightningStrike the draft Lightning strikes a pencil wireframe and the finished design spreads out from every bolt. Click to call down your own. Open on its own → 06FrostShatter the draft Frost creeps over a pencil wireframe until it is frozen solid, then the ice cracks and falls away in shards to reveal the finished design. Click to freeze a patch and shatter it. Open on its own →

Scroll back up in any chapter and the draft comes back. Each element also has a page of its own.

The shinobi pack Use it in your project

Raw WebGL2 and canvas, no libraries. Without WebGL2 it falls back to a wipe. With reduced motion on it cross-fades instead, with no flashes. Sound is off until you turn it on, and every sound is synthesised in the browser with Web Audio, so there are no audio files. One WebGL canvas draws every chapter, each with its own fragment shader.
