Shadow Clone

The sketch starts to tremble and its lines double, a shadow of itself pulling away. Then it splits: a puff of smoke covers the page and nine clones fly out of it, each a whole copy of the draft, stacked and overlapping.

Keep scrolling and the clone on top goes up in smoke, showing whatever is under it, one after another, until only the finished design is left.

Click a clone to dispel it early. Scroll back up and they come back. With sound on, every clone goes with a poof. Scroll back up and the draft comes back.
