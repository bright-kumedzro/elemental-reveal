Lightning Blade

The light drains from the page and a dense ball of blue-white lightning gathers low on the left, held down by the paper like a hand. It shrieks like a flock of birds while arcs spray off it and crawl over the paper, which scorches underneath.

Then it charges across the page, dragged along the paper, gouging a burning trench with sparks spraying off the back, and thrusts straight through at the end with a flash. The trench widens as you scroll, its edge white-hot and flickering, with cracks of light running ahead of it.

Move over the paper and static gathers under your hand. Click to call down a bolt that blows its own hole. With sound on, the shriek climbs as it charges and holds through the run, and the pierce lands with a thunderclap. Scroll back up and the draft comes back.
