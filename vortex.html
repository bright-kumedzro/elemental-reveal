Sand Vortex

Sand drifts in from the corners and grains lift off the darkest pencil strokes. Then a storm swirls round the page and wears the sketch away from the outside in, grain by grain along spiral arms, the paper going gritty ahead of it.

Behind it the finished design shows through the swirling haze. At the end the storm closes into a ball of sand in the middle, and the ball bursts: the sand rains off the page and the air clears.

Move over the paper and the sand eddies round your hand. Click to stir up a whirl that wears its own hole. With sound on, the storm roars and hisses, and the burst lands with a whump. Scroll back up and the draft comes back.
