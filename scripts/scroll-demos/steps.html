Scroll · Steps

A story told in steps. One visual stays put while the steps scroll past, and it changes with each one.

01 · Discover
02 · Define
03 · Design
04 · Deliver

  1. 01Discover

    Talk to the people who'll use it, and to the people who'll pay for it. Collect what hurts, what's been tried and what success would look like.

  2. 02Define

    Turn it into one problem worth solving, a way to measure it, and what's out of scope. Everyone signs up to the same target.

  3. 03Design

    Sketch, prototype and test the riskiest part first. Wireframes become a clickable flow, then a design system the team can build from.

  4. 04Deliver

    Ship the first complete cycle, measure it against the target, and keep improving what's live instead of polishing what isn't.
