Sandstorm

From the first scroll the wind is up: sand races across the page in gusts, bands of haze drift past, the paper shivers and the light goes orange-brown and dim. On the left a towering wall of dust looms, rust-dark at its base and wispy up high, its billows rolling forward.

It bears down and sweeps across. Inside it you can barely see the page: streaks of sand race past and tear the sketch off grain by grain. Behind it the haze slowly thins, and the finished design is left standing.

Move over the paper and the sand eddies round your hand. Click to kick up a dust devil that scours its own hole. With sound on, the wind howls and gusts from the first scroll, and the sand hisses harder the faster you scroll. Scroll back up and the draft comes back.
