Five ways to lose a draft

Every chapter holds the same two drawings: a pencil wireframe on paper and the finished design underneath. A WebGL2 shader gives each pixel a fuel value and decides, pixel by pixel, which drawing you see. Scrolling pushes the front through the fuel. Each element shapes that fuel differently and does different damage at the edge.

Fire · Burn the draft
The first scroll calls down a comet. It streaks in trailing fire, lights up the page as it comes, and hits: a flash, a shockwave that bends everything it crosses, and a blast crater that catches. From there the fire spreads as you scroll. Ahead of the fire the paper browns and chars. Flames rise off the burning edge, torn into tongues by heat rising through them. Embers lift away, and the finished design wavers in the heat behind it. Move over the paper and fire follows your hand. Click and a fireball arcs up from below and starts a new fire where it lands.
Water · Wash the draft
Water pours in from the top and runs down the page. Each rivulet swells to a rounded head and widens until it merges with its neighbours. The paper ahead wicks the water up and greys, and the pencil runs. In the water, ripples flow downhill and bend the finished design underneath. The sketch’s pigment washes out just behind the front. Click the paper to spill some: it pools where it lands, then runs down.
Air · Scatter the draft
Wind comes in from the left. The paper flutters before the gust arrives. Then the wind strips it off in streaks and carries it away. Each paper fleck keeps the colour of the bit of sketch it tore from. Click the paper to send a gust: it blows a hole outward from where you clicked.
Earth · Break the draft
Cracks race out from the epicentre along jagged fault lines, with hairlines branching off them. Each chunk works loose and rattles, then breaks away and falls down the page, turning as it drops. Behind each one is a shadowed hole with the finished design at the bottom. Scroll fast and the ground shakes. Click the paper to start a new quake.
Lightning · Strike the draft
Four bolts grow as branching random walks, and each one gets its own distance field. When your scroll reaches a bolt, it strikes. The bolt flashes, and the reveal spreads out from the channel it burned. Click the paper to call down your own bolt.

Scroll back up in any chapter and the draft comes back. Each element also has a page of its own.

Use it in your project More on the playground

Raw WebGL2 and canvas, no libraries. Without WebGL2 it falls back to a wipe. With reduced motion on it cross-fades instead, with no flashes. One WebGL canvas draws all five chapters, each with its own fragment shader.
