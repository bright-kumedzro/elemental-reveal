Sand Tornado

The light goes grey and a thin rope of sand reaches down from a dust cloud at the top of the page. It touches down in the middle with a thud and a burst of dust, then grows as you scroll, wider, taller and nearer, its bands of sand getting bigger as it comes.

Its foot tears the paper away from the middle outward, along an edge that turns with it, and the scraps spiral up into the column. By the end it fills the screen and blows through in a blast of dust, and when the air clears only the finished design is left.

Move over the paper and it swirls under your hand. Click to spin up a dust devil that tears its own patch. With sound on, the twister roars like a freight train, louder the nearer it gets and the harder you scroll, and lands with a thud. Scroll back up and the draft comes back.
