Spiral Sphere

A sphere of light gathers in the middle of the page. Streams of light spiral into it, and the paper around it is dragged round into the spin. When it hits there is a flash and a shockwave, and it grinds into the page.

The hole spreads as you scroll. Its edge saws round on spinning spiral arms, scoring and twisting the paper ahead of it. Behind it the finished design settles out of the twist, with rings of light running out from the sphere.

Move over the paper and it twists under your hand. Click to drop a small sphere that drills its own hole. With sound on, the sphere whirs louder and higher as it charges, and grinds as it drills. Scroll back up and the draft comes back.
